🚇 Rail Rush

Access

With spectators on, anyone can watch and the password is only needed to join a team. With it off, the password is needed to open the game at all.

Game

“Get here!” reward (coins)

Challenge pacing

Maximum on the board
When a challenge is finished it is replaced by up to 2 new ones, never going over the limit. With the limit off the board keeps growing.
Challenges at the start
Used when the game starts and after “Reset all challenges”.
Expire after (minutes)
A challenge still on the board after that long goes back in the bank (shots called and fails are cleared) and one new challenge goes live.

Reset stations

Sets every station back to 0 for both teams. History is kept.

Reset balances

Sets both teams' coin balances to 0.

Reset challenges

Starts the challenge deck over: clears every result and called shot, returns all challenges to the bank, and puts 3 new challenges and a new Get here! on the map. Balances and stations are not changed.

Network

End game

Freezes the game for everyone and saves it to Past Games for every player who joined (and for you). This cannot be undone.
